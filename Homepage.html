Questions

QN-1: How to book an appointment?
Ans: Click "Start Now" or visit the Providers page.

QN-2: Is telemedicine safe?
Ans: Yes, all sessions are private and secure.

QN-3: How do I get my prescription?
Ans: Your doctor will upload it to your dashboard.

QN-4: Can I choose my own doctor?
Ans: Yes, you can browse doctor profiles in the Providers section.

QN-5: What if I miss my appointment?
Ans: You can reschedule from your dashboard settings.

QN-6: Are lab reports available online?
Ans: Yes, visit the Medical Records section to view them.

QN-7: How much does a session cost?
Ans: Prices vary by doctor; check their profile for details.

QN-8: Do you offer 24/7 support?
Ans: Yes, our emergency hotline is open 24 hours.

QN-9: Can I use insurance?
Ans: We accept most major health insurance providers.

QN-10: How do I pay?
Ans: You can pay via credit card, debit card, or online banking.

QN-11: Can I see a specialist?
Ans: Yes, we have specialists for various medical fields.

QN-12: How long is a session?
Ans: Typical sessions last between 15 to 30 minutes.

QN-13: Is my data protected?
Ans: We follow strict data protection laws to keep your info safe.

QN-14: Can I cancel a booking?
Ans: Yes, you can cancel up to 2 hours before the time.

QN-15: Do you provide international services?
Ans: Yes, our platform is accessible from anywhere.
