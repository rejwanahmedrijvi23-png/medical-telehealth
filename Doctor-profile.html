Dr. Alexandar Wright
Specialist in Cardiology - MBBS, FCPS

Biography

Dr. Alexander Wright is an internationally acclaimed Cardiologist with over 15 years of clinical experience. He earned his MD from Harvard Medical School and holds prestigious MRCP and FRCP degrees from the Royal College of Physicians, London. Specializing in Interventional Cardiology, he has served as a Senior Consultant at St Bartholomew's Hospital, UK, and Mount Elizabeth Hospital, Singapore. His expertise lies in advanced heart failure management, angioplasty, and preventive cardiac care. Known for his patient-centric approach, Dr. Wright now leverages global telemedicine to provide world-class heart treatments to patients worldwide.

Neurology Specialist - Senior Consultant

He earned his PhD in Neurology from the University of Vienna and holds a fellowship from the European Board of Neurology.
